Hue House

Mix colors. Restore homes.

A calm color puzzle. Pour paint from jar to jar, mix the primaries into the colors a client ordered, then roll the paint onto their house, one wall, sofa and cabinet at a time. When a house is finished, a new client moves in.

Your progress stays on your phone

There is no account. Your levels, stars, coins, painted houses and settings are saved on your iPhone and nowhere else, and the game plays without internet (the ads need it).

Deleting the app deletes your progress for good. It isn’t part of iCloud or computer backups, so it can’t be brought back, and a new phone starts from level 1.

Questions

I’m stuck on a level. What can I do?

Undo takes back the last pour, Hint shows the next pour of a way to finish, and Restart starts the level over. Undo and Hint are free a few times a level, then you can watch a short ad for more; Restart is always free. If you reach a point with no way on, the game tells you. Every level can be solved.

How do I make a color?

Pour two different paints into the Mixer, one unit at a time: red and yellow make orange, yellow and blue make green, red and blue make purple, and white makes lighter shades. Tap a color on the client’s order to see every step of making it.

Some colors are hard for me to tell apart.

Turn on Settings › Color symbols. Every paint then carries a shape of its own as well as its color.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

In Settings › Sound and feel. Sound and music each have their own volume, too. The game stays quiet when your iPhone is on silent.

My phone gets warm, or the battery drains fast.

In Settings, set Graphics to Low and Frame rate to 60 or 30. With Graphics on Auto, the effects turn off by themselves in Low Power Mode.

How do I start over?

Settings › Reset progress. It clears your levels, coins and painted houses. Your settings and the skins you bought stay.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not at the moment. Progress lives only on the phone you play on.

Is there anything to buy?

Not with real money. Coins are earned by playing, and they buy skins in the shop and a little extra help in levels.

Why are there ads?

They keep the game free. Now and then an ad plays between levels, and you can choose to watch one for an extra undo, hint or more moves. The ads come from Google and are limited to ones suitable for all ages.

It says “No ad right now”.

Ads need the internet, and sometimes Google has none to show. Check your connection and try again a little later, or restart the level, which is always free.

Why does the game ask about tracking or consent?

Apple’s prompt asks whether the ads may use your phone’s advertising identifier, and in Europe, the UK and Switzerland Google’s form asks what the ads may do. Saying no to tracking only makes the ads less tailored to you. To change your answer, use your iPhone’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or Settings › Ad privacy choices in the game where it is shown.

What data does the game collect?

The game itself has no account and sends nothing to us. Google’s ads collect some data about your device and the ads you see, to show them. The privacy policy has the details.

Get in touch
