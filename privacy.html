Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Hue House for iPhone, version 1.0

The short version

The game
Keeps your progress on your phone, needs no account, and sends nothing to us.
The ads
Come from Google, which collects some data about your device and the ads you see in order to show them. They use your advertising identifier only if you allow it.

What the game keeps on your phone

To remember your game between visits, Hue House saves a few things in its own storage on your phone:

  • Your progress: the level you are on, your stars, your coins, the paint in each client’s house, and the skins you have bought and wear.
  • Your settings: your choices in Settings, such as sound, music, vibration, light or dark look, graphics, frame rate and color symbols.
  • A few levels made for you: later levels are made on your phone as you play, and the last few are kept so they don’t have to be made again.

This is used only to run the game. It never leaves your phone, and we cannot see it. It is not part of your iCloud or computer backups.

What the game looks at, but doesn’t keep

To pick graphics that suit your phone, the game checks whether Low Power Mode and Reduce Motion are on, roughly how new your phone is and how much memory it has, and how fast its screen refreshes. It uses this on the spot. It doesn’t store it or send it anywhere.

The only permission the game may ask for is Apple’s tracking permission, for the ads (see below). It doesn’t use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location, and it sends no notifications. Its sounds and music are files inside the app.

Ads by Google

Hue House is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: now and then an ad between levels, and ads you choose to watch for an extra undo, hint or more moves. To choose, show and count these ads, and to find problems in them, Google’s ad software in the game collects:

  • Identifiers: your phone’s advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and other identifiers that are limited to this app or its developer.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address. Never your precise location.
  • How you use the ads and the app: the ads you see, taps, video views and app launches.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data, such as how long the app takes to start.

Google uses this to show and measure ads and for analytics, and may share it with other companies that show ads. It handles this data under its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We only see totals from Google, such as how many ads were shown, never data about you.

Ads are limited to ones suitable for all ages. Ads need the internet; the game doesn’t.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking: the game may ask once, with Apple’s prompt, whether it may track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, Google gets no advertising identifier and the ads are less tailored to you. You see the same number of ads either way. To change your answer, go to your iPhone’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks you first what the ads may do. To change your choices, open Settings › Ad privacy choices in the game.
  • Ads for help are optional: you never have to watch one. Restarting a level is always free.

What we collect ourselves: nothing

Hue House has no account or sign-in and asks for no personal information. Apart from Google’s ads, it contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and it sends nothing to us. There is nothing to buy with real money: coins are earned by playing.

We don’t sell anything about you, and there is no data about you on any server of ours.

Apple

You get Hue House from the App Store, which is run by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may show us crash reports and overall usage numbers that don’t identify you. We don’t receive anything else.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and everything it kept on your phone is deleted with it, for good, including the choices you made about ads.

To start the game over without deleting it, open Settings › Reset progress. That clears your levels, coins and painted houses, and keeps your settings and skins.

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete, correct or send you. For data Google collected through the ads, see the Google Privacy Policy linked above. You are welcome to ask us anything about it.

Children’s privacy

Hue House is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, its ads are limited to ones suitable for all ages, and nobody can sign up, chat, or share anything in it.

This website

These pages use no cookies, analytics or scripts, and load nothing from other sites. The company that hosts them may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes what the game or its ads collect, we will update this page before that update comes out and change the date at the top. The update’s notes in the App Store will say so, and where the law or Apple requires your permission, the game will ask for it first.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or about anything else in the game: write to huehouse.game@icloud.com.
